Our Approach

A specialized approach to the upper cervical spine

The upper cervical spine possesses a biometric profile fundamentally different from the rest of the spinal column. While most chiropractors apply a “one-size-fits-all” approach — adjusting the neck with the same general force and mechanics used for the lower back — they often fail to account for these unique biometrics. Because the upper neck is the most delicate and complex area of the nervous system, it requires a level of precision that standard chiropractic training simply doesn’t cover.

Meet the Team

Trained by the pioneers. Refined over 30 years.

Dr. Doble represents the elite tier of upper cervical care. While many practitioners attempt these adjustments after only a weekend seminar, Dr. Doble committed to a seven-year “hyprentorship” to master the craft. His pedigree is unmatched, having been trained directly by the industry’s most respected pioneers: Dr. Clarence Jenson, Dr. Brian Duff and Dr. Latch.

Drawing from over 30 years of experience, Dr. Doble has synthesized the best of two worlds. He was originally inspired by the Palmer Toggle Recoil Method after it cured his own chronic migraines, leading him to spend years mastering the technique under Drs. Duff and Jenson. He later integrated the Orthospinology method of Dr. Cecil Laney and Dr. Nathan Berner.

Today, Dr. Doble uses a proprietary system that “marries” these methods — offering both a highly refined manual Palmer Toggle Recoil adjustment and the precision of the Laney hand-held instrument. As a researcher, educator and co-author of a chiropractic textbook, he provides a level of safety and clinical accuracy that only comes from decades of dedicated specialization.
