The Nervous System Controls All Functions

The nervous system controls every function and process of your body, including every cell, affecting your total health. If the nervous system is interfered with, then dysfunction will occur in the body causing symptoms and chronic conditions to manifest over time. A misalignment in the upper cervical region will cause interference to the nervous system.

Restricted or Distorted Brain Messages

When head/neck misalignment develops and the body becomes imbalanced, the brain stem becomes irritated, restricting or distorting the flow of healing messages from the brain to that part of the body serviced by these irritated nerves. This can cause pain and suffering and decrease the quality of your life.

Here's How —

The re-positioning of the upper cervical vertebra is done by hand or by an instrument. Depending on what will work best for the patient, it feels like a light quick tap on the side of the neck as the vertebra is moved precisely back into its correct position. It is completely "underwhelming."
