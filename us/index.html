Chiropractor

Richard Doble D.C.
The Neck Doctor

Since 1991, Dr. Richard Doble, D.C. has had the privilege of treating thousands of patients at his clinics. He started his practice in San Francisco before moving to Sebastopol. He has helped many individuals achieve wellness without medication through a specialized cervical adjustment known as Toggle Recoil.

Dr. Doble's journey into chiropractic began in his teenage years. A series of neck and back injuries from wrestling, bodybuilding, powerlifting, and manual labor led him to discover chiropractic care. After unsuccessful treatments by numerous medical doctors, he found relief through a chiropractor, realizing that chiropractic offered solutions where traditional medicine did not.

After studying chiropractic at Life Chiropractic College West Dr. Doble gained extensive post-graduate training under renowned chiropractors. While exploring various chiropractic techniques, Dr. Doble received a life-changing adjustment from Dr. Bryan Duff using the Palmer Method of Upper Cervical Chiropractic. This single Toggle Recoil adjustment alleviated his chronic migraine headaches. Inspired by the effectiveness of this technique, Dr. Doble sought to master it himself.

In 1999, Dr. Doble found Dr. Clarence Jenson from Carmichael, California, who directly learned from Dr. B.J. Palmer, the developer of chiropractic, in the 1950s. Under Dr. Jenson's mentorship, Dr. Doble spent five years becoming an expert in the B.J. Palmer Upper Cervical HIO method.

Specific Upper Cervical Chiropractic Care was the only treatment that fully eradicated his pain. Dr. Doble's personal triumphs in chiropractic care have molded his approach to wellness, emphasizing honest and specific treatment. He is passionate and dedicated to practicing chiropractic correctly. Consulting with thousands of patients and experiencing the natural aging process himself, Dr. Doble has developed an instinctive expertise in his field.

Believing in the mantra "movement is life," Dr. Doble strives to help everyone achieve peak wellness.

"When Love and Skill Work Together, Expect a Masterpiece."

Dr. B.J. Palmer
