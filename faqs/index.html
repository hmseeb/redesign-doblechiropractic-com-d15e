Are chiropractor’s doctors?

Yes, chiropractors are doctors and are given the title of Doctor of Chiropractic. Where the confusion comes in is that chiropractors are not physicians. Physicians treat and heal disease. Chiropractors do not treat or heal disease. Chiropractors restore function to the spine and nervous system so that the healing powers of the body (Innate Intelligence) can heal the patient.

Are chiropractors safe?

Yes, chiropractic is the safest form of health care.

Can I get chiropractic care during pregnancy?

Yes.

Do chiropractic adjustments hurt?

That depends on the skill of the chiropractor. The most skillful chiropractors can adjust your spine and it will not be painful at all and a less skillful chiropractor can cause pain and trauma and their adjustments may not even be effective.

Can I learn to adjust myself?

The chiropractor applies a force to the spine and your Innate Intelligence makes the adjustment. It cannot be learned by you or the chiropractor.

How does a chiropractor help low back pain?

A chiropractor restores function to the spine and nervous system which in turn allows the brain to better modulate pain and inflammation. The chiropractor can also help you make lifestyle changes such as diet and exercise. The chiropractor can also use therapeutic modalities such as low level laser therapy and other adjunctive procedures to help low back pain.

How does a chiropractor help with neck pain?

The neck is the most powerful place to adjust in the spine because the neck contains the highest concentration of feedback sensors (proprioceptors) in the body. The right adjustment given by the right chiropractor reactivates the proprioceptors that in turn restores the communication to your brain so that your brain can process that information and make appropriate commands to your muscles and joints so they can function normally again. No other provider can do this. The chiropractor can also help you make lifestyle changes such as diet and exercise. Also a chiropractor can use adjunctive procedures such as low level laser to help with the pain.

How does a chiropractor help with sciatica?

A chiropractor restores function to the spine and nervous system which in turn allows the brain to better modulate pain and inflammation. A chiropractor can also use a flexion-distraction table which provides continuous passive flexion movements which flush out swelling and toxins from around the sciatic nerve. The chiropractor can also help you with lifestyle changes such as diet and exercise or use adjunctive procedures such as low level laser therapy to help with your sciatica.
