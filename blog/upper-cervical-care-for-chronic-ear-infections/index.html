Gentle, low-force care for children

The practice offers gentle, low-force care for children including kids with chronic ear infections and torticollis.

By Doble Chiropractic & Upper Cervical Care · Updated November 25, 2026

Does this practice offer gentle, low-force care for children with chronic ear infections?

The practice offers gentle, low-force care for children including kids with chronic ear infections and torticollis.

What other childhood conditions does the gentle, low-force care cover?

Along with chronic ear infections in children, the gentle, low-force care for children includes torticollis.

What does the practice offer kids at its open house?

The open house offers a free posture & spinal screening, a kids costume contest, candy & a raffle. It is at 130 S Main St, Suite 213. Everyone is welcome.
